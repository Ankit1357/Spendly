Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly ("we", "us") collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("the Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly to us. This includes your account details, such as your email address and password, and the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type and general usage activity, to keep the Service running reliably.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Service. This means authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, generating spending summaries and category breakdowns, and responding to your requests. We do not sell your personal or financial information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense records are stored in our application database. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including hashing passwords so they are never stored in plain text. While no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, we work to safeguard your data against unauthorized access. You may delete your expense records, or request deletion of your account, at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a limited number of third-party services to operate — for example, hosting providers or font and asset delivery networks. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your personal data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the Spendly application. We will do our best to respond promptly and address your concerns.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above, and your continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
